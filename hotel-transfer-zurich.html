VIP hotel transfer

The ride should feel like part of the hotel

A five-star stay deserves a five-star arrival. Our luxury hotel transfer in Zurich brings you from the airport to your suite without friction: your chauffeur tracks the flight, greets you at arrivals and hands you over — rested — to the hotel doorman.

During your stay, your hotel chauffeur remains at your disposal: dinners in the old town, meetings across Zurich, day trips to Lucerne or St. Moritz. We work with the concierge desks of the city's finest houses, so every departure is timed to the minute.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which hotels in Zurich do you serve?
All of them — from Baur au Lac, The Dolder Grand and Park Hyatt to boutique houses and hotels outside the city. Wherever you stay, your chauffeur is at the entrance.
Can my hotel concierge book the transfer for me?
Yes. Concierge desks can book directly by phone, WhatsApp or email — we confirm within minutes and coordinate the pickup with the front desk.
Do you offer transfers between hotels in different cities?
Of course. We drive intercity hotel transfers across Switzerland — Zurich to St. Moritz, Lucerne, Geneva or Zermatt — door to door, at a fixed price.
How is luggage handled?
Your chauffeur takes care of everything from the lobby to the car boot, and hands your luggage to the hotel porter on arrival. You never touch a suitcase.
