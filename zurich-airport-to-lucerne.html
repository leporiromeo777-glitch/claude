The route

The lake and mountains, 50 minutes away

Lucerne is the closest of Switzerland's postcard towns — about 67 km and 50 minutes from Zurich Airport. Your chauffeur takes you door to door: no train changes with luggage, no queues.

Many guests add a scenic stop on the way or book the return with a sightseeing detour around Lake Lucerne — ask when you book and we plan it into the ride.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does the transfer from Zurich Airport to Lucerne take?
About 50 min door to door in normal traffic, over 67 km. Your chauffeur plans the departure around your flight and the day's conditions.
How much does a transfer to Lucerne cost?
You get a fixed quote when you book — agreed before departure, no meter, no hidden extras. The price depends on the vehicle class you choose.
What happens if my flight is delayed?
Nothing changes for you: we track the flight live and adjust the pickup automatically, at no extra cost.
Can we stop for sightseeing on the way?
Of course. Tell us when booking and we plan a scenic stop — or turn the transfer into an hourly booking and keep the car for the day.
