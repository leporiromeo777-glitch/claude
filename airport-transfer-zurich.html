VIP airport transfer

From the runway to your door, in total comfort

Landing in Zurich after a long flight, the last thing you want is a taxi queue. With our VIP airport transfer in Zurich, a professional chauffeur tracks your flight, waits for you at arrivals with a name board and takes you straight to your hotel, office or home in a Mercedes S-Class, E-Class or V-Class.

We also handle departures: your driver picks you up on time, helps with your luggage and drops you off directly at the right terminal at Zurich Airport. For groups and delegations we provide V-Class vans and Sprinter minibuses for up to 16 passengers.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the airport meet and greet in Zurich work?
Your chauffeur waits inside the arrivals hall at Zurich Airport holding a board with your name, helps with your luggage and walks you to the car. No searching for a driver in the parking lot.
What happens if my flight is delayed?
Nothing changes for you. We track your flight live and automatically adjust the pickup time, at no extra cost — your driver is there when you land.
How much does an airport transfer in Zurich cost?
You receive a fixed price when you book, agreed before departure. Traffic and normal waiting time never change it — no hidden fees.
Can I book an airport transfer for a group?
Yes. Alongside our sedans we offer the Mercedes V-Class for up to 7 passengers and Sprinter minibuses for up to 16 — ideal for delegations and events.
