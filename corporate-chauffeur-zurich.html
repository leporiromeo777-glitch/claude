FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer corporate accounts with monthly invoicing?
Yes. Companies can open a corporate account with monthly invoicing, priority booking and a dedicated contact person for all their transfers in Zurich and beyond.
Can you organise a roadshow with several stops?
Absolutely. We plan multi-stop itineraries, coordinate several vehicles if needed and keep your delegation on schedule for the whole day.
How discreet are your drivers?
Discretion is part of our training. Conversations, documents and guests remain strictly confidential — our executive drivers are used to working with VIP clients.
Can I book the same driver for recurring business transfers?
Yes, on request we assign the same chauffeur to your company for regular transfers, so your team always sees a familiar face.
