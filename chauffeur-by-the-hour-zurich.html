Your schedule is the itinerary

Some days do not fit into A-to-B rides: three meetings across town, a fitting on Bahnhofstrasse, dinner at the lake. With a private chauffeur by the hour in Zurich, one driver and one car stay with you the whole time — you step out, the car waits, you move on.

For longer programmes, our full day chauffeur service covers up to 12 hours with one dedicated driver: sightseeing in the Alps, a wedding day, a client visit across several cities. You set the pace, we handle the road.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum booking for a chauffeur by the hour?
Two hours. From there you can extend hour by hour — even during the ride, if your day runs longer than planned.
What is included in the hourly rate?
The chauffeur, the vehicle, fuel and waiting time at every stop. You pay one transparent rate agreed at booking — no hidden extras.
Can I book a chauffeur for a full day?
Yes. Our full day chauffeur service gives you one dedicated driver and vehicle for up to 12 hours, in Zurich or anywhere in Switzerland.
Can the route change during the booking?
Completely. With hourly service the itinerary is yours: add stops, change destinations or extend the time — your chauffeur adapts on the spot.
