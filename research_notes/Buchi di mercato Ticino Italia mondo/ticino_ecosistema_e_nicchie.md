# Ticino: startup/new-business ecosystem and sector niches for a small new business (2025-2026)

Research date: 14 Oct 2026. Data year is marked when it is not 2025-2026. Some figures come from search-engine summaries of the pages cited; the URL listed is the page the figure is attributed to.

## Q1. Support ecosystem: who helps, and with what grants/incentives (startups, new self-employed, takeovers)

### Takeaway
Ticino has a dense support system, but it is aimed mainly at *innovative/scalable* startups (Agire, Tecnopolo, Boldbrain, TiVentures, L-Inn, tax breaks for startups). For a "normal" small business (trade, services, shop), the most concrete cash support is the cantonal **L-rilocc** self-employment aid: up to 100% of the owner's AVS/AI/IPG contributions for 24 months, plus a loss guarantee. The regional development agencies (ERS) also give small grants or loans. Takeover/succession support is mostly information (ti.ch/trasmissione-aziendale) rather than money.

### Cited Findings
**Cantonal map of startup support (official slides, DFE Portale impresa, Nov 2025)**
- The Canton calls itself "the second innovation ecosystem in Switzerland". It lists these actors: Innosuisse, Fondazione AGIRE, Tecnopolo Ticino, Boldbrain Startup Challenge, USI Startup Centre, Impact Hub Ticino, TiVentures, Switzerland Innovation Park Ticino and the Canton — [DFE slides "Specific Measures for Innovative Startups" (2025)](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DE-PORTALE-IMPRESA/documenti/20251105_slide_startup_sito_ENG.pdf)
- **Innosuisse**: "Business Concept" and "Business Growth" courses for future entrepreneurs, plus vouchers for coaching by nationally accredited coaches for young firms — [same slides](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DE-PORTALE-IMPRESA/documenti/20251105_slide_startup_sito_ENG.pdf)
- **Fondazione AGIRE**: the cantonal innovation agency and "first entry point to the regional innovation system". It gives individual and group consulting to "highly innovative startups" and runs "Scouting for Investors" pitch events. It also manages **Tecnopolo Ticino** (offices, tools, networking for young innovative firms, in Manno) — [same slides](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DE-PORTALE-IMPRESA/documenti/20251105_slide_startup_sito_ENG.pdf)
- **Boldbrain Startup Challenge**: an early-stage accelerator run by Agire with the USI Startup Centre. It offers 3 months of free coaching, workshops on the idea, business plan and pitch, and cash/in-kind prizes — [slides](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DE-PORTALE-IMPRESA/documenti/20251105_slide_startup_sito_ENG.pdf). The 2025 call was open 16 Apr–5 Jun 2025 to applicants "from all of Switzerland and beyond" — [Agire](https://agire.ch/novita-eventi/boldbrain-aperta-la-call-ledizione-2025). The 2024 (7th) edition had 20 startups, 11 of them from Ticino, and CHF 120,000 in cash prizes — [Greater Zurich Area](https://www.greaterzuricharea.com/en/news/boldbrain-challenge-2024-supports-20-startups)
- **USI Startup Centre**: an academic incubator/accelerator offering hosting, consulting and matchmaking with academic expertise — [slides](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DE-PORTALE-IMPRESA/documenti/20251105_slide_startup_sito_ENG.pdf); see also [USI "Launch your startup in Ticino"](https://www.startup.usi.ch/en/feeds/40064)
- **Impact Hub Ticino**: coworking, programmes and events for freelancers, SMEs, startups, non-profits and social enterprises, with a focus on sustainability, social innovation and the circular economy — [slides](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DE-PORTALE-IMPRESA/documenti/20251105_slide_startup_sito_ENG.pdf)
- **TiVentures**: a seed fund for early-stage deeptech in health, high-tech, digital and cleantech — [slides](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DE-PORTALE-IMPRESA/documenti/20251105_slide_startup_sito_ENG.pdf)
- **Cantonal tax breaks for innovative startups** (art. 5a of the cantonal tax regulation):
  - The startup is exempt from donation tax, and its capital tax is reduced to 0.01‰.
  - Private investors in recognised startups have the invested amount taxed separately at 1%.
  - Patent box relief of up to 90%, an extra R&D deduction of up to 50%, and total relief capped at 70% of taxable profit.
  - Source: [slides](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DE-PORTALE-IMPRESA/documenti/20251105_slide_startup_sito_ENG.pdf). The certificate is issued by UAC — [ti.ch UAC](https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/de/uac/agevolazioni-fiscali/rilascio-attestazioni-per-agevolazioni-fiscali-per-start-up-innovative)
- **L-Inn (Legge per l'innovazione economica, 2015)**: aid goes to "private companies oriented to export, with high growth potential and relevant economic spillovers", as tax relief and/or non-repayable grants. Firms must also meet salary and resident-employment criteria (executive decrees and forms for 2025) — [ti.ch UAC](https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/de/uac/versamento-dei-sussidi/legge-per-linnovazione-economica)
- **New L-Inn SME measure**: a 25% contribution to the cost of a research mandate given to a recognised Swiss research centre, up to CHF 50,000 per project (projects up to CHF 200,000) — [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Innovazione-fino-a-50%E2%80%99000-franchi-per-le-piccole-e-medie-imprese--3294452.html); [SUPSI](https://www.supsi.ch/nuove-misure-a-sostegno-dell-innovazione-nelle-pmi); [ti.ch press release](https://m4.ti.ch/tich/area-media/comunicati/dettaglio-comunicato?NEWS_ID=256297&cHash=064afabdf1e89d4dcc50b13957edecea)
- **L-rilocc (Legge sul rilancio dell'occupazione e sul sostegno ai disoccupati), art. 12, for new self-employed activities**:
  - The State can pay **100% of the owner's mandatory AVS/AI/IPG contributions for up to 24 months**.
  - For particularly innovative projects, it can guarantee up to 20% of losses, capped at CHF 180,000.
  - It can provide a specialised consultant in year 1.
  - The application must be filed **within 30 days of starting** the activity, and the aid covers only activity carried out in Ticino.
  - Sources: [lexfind law text](https://www.lexfind.ch/tolv/116703/it); [L-rilocc report 2020](https://m4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DE-SDL/b_pubblicazioni/rilocc/RapportoLRilocc2020.pdf)
  - Eligibility in the 2018 report: unemployed people, or people threatened with unemployment, resident in Ticino for at least 1 year — [Report 2018](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DE-SDL/b_pubblicazioni/rilocc/RapportoLRilocc2018.pdf). Since a 2008 amendment, the aid is also open to people not registered as unemployed — [GC report 9311](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/POTERI/GC/allegati/rapporti/9311_6867R.pdf)
  - A proposed change (24 months of social-charge aid, a 30% guarantee, reimbursement of training courses) appears in parliamentary doc 9311. Its approval status is unverified.
- **Scale of the self-employment aid**: a 2019 cantonal speech said Ticino supports on average more than 80 self-employment activities a year, for about CHF 3 million in total — [ti.ch speech 2019](https://www3.ti.ch/DFE/discorsi/17-01-2019-discorso-29563476.pdf) (2019 data)
- **ERS (Enti regionali per lo sviluppo)**: there are 4 ERS (Luganese, Mendrisiotto, Bellinzonese e Valli, Locarnese e Vallemaggia), under the cantonal law applying federal regional policy (NPR, in force 1 Oct 2009). For 2020-2023, each ERS received CHF 1.5M for projects, plus up to CHF 0.5M for peripheral areas. They give grants or loans, and each ERS sets its own criteria — [ti.ch Presentazione](https://www3.ti.ch/COMUNICAZIONI/224292/Presentazione.pdf); [laRegione, ERS topic](https://www.laregione.ch/temi/ente-regionale-sviluppo); [regiosuisse 2012-15 programme](https://regiosuisse.ch/sites/default/files/2016-10/up-ti-2012-2015.pdf) (older)
- **BancaStato** added another CHF 5M for Ticino startups in 2022 — [laRegione](https://www.laregione.ch/cantone/ticino/1627350/anni-start-up-franchi-aziende-innovazione) (2022)
- **Ecosystem size**: an EY barometer counted **20 financing rounds in Ticino in 2025**, behind Zurich, Romandy, Central and North-West Switzerland. Swiss startups raised CHF 3.3bn in total in 2025 — [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/various/almost-3-3-billion-invested-in-swiss-start-ups/90987711)
- Tecnopolo metrics from a secondary profile site (unverified): more than CHF 85M invested in Tecnopolo companies since opening, 90% occupancy at end 2024, 124 active startups in the 2024 portfolio (97 in 2023) — [yespress.io](https://yespress.io/fondazione-agire-tecnopolo-ticino)
- **Business succession support**: the Canton directs owners to the [ti.ch/trasmissione-aziendale](https://www4.ti.ch/dfe/de/portale-impresa/economic-development/insights/business-succession) portal.

### Inferences
- A non-innovative small business (cleaning, trades, home care, digital agency) will get little from Agire, Tecnopolo, L-Inn or TiVentures, which target export-oriented, scalable firms. Its realistic aids are:
  - L-rilocc: 24 months of AVS paid. At a CHF 60k income that is about CHF 6,000 a year, so roughly CHF 12k in total.
  - An ERS grant or loan, mainly in peripheral valleys.
  - Innosuisse coaching vouchers.
- The 30-day filing deadline for L-rilocc is an easy trap. A founder should contact the Sezione del lavoro *before* registering the business.

### Gaps
- **GENILEM** is a Romandy (Geneva/Vaud/Fribourg/Valais/Neuchâtel/Jura) incubator. I found no evidence that it operates in Ticino.
- **Swiss Startup Association**: I found nothing Ticino-specific.
- **Microcredit**: I found no Ticino-specific microcredit programme in this research. It may exist through Microcrédit Solidaire Suisse or similar, but this was not verified.
- **"Fondo cantonale"** for startups: I found no fund with that name. The relevant fund is TiVentures (seed, deeptech).
- Current ERS grant amounts and calls for 2024-2027 were not found; the figures above are 2020-2023.
- No official 2025 Agire annual figures were found.

## Q2. Practical setup: costs, legal form, social contributions, taxes, permits, living-cost threshold

### Takeaway
A sole proprietorship (*ditta individuale*) costs roughly CHF 0–1,500 to set up. A Sagl/GmbH costs about CHF 2,000–6,000 plus CHF 20,000 of paid-up capital. Self-employed AVS/AI/IPG is 10% of income above CHF 60,500, with a minimum of CHF 530 a year (2025). The overall corporate profit tax burden in Ticino is about 18.6%. EU citizens can be self-employed in Ticino as cross-border workers (G permit, 5 years). Ticino wages are about 19% below the Swiss median while health premiums are the highest in Switzerland. Both points matter for founder income needs and for customers' purchasing power.

### Cited Findings
- **Sagl setup cost**: about CHF 3,000–6,000 in total, made up of notarial acts CHF 700–2,000, commercial register CHF 400–600, and advice/other CHF 600–2,000. Minimum capital is CHF 20,000, fully paid in — [PostFinance](https://www.postfinance.ch/it/blog/blog-sul-business/quanto-costa-costituire-unimpresa.html). Helvetia puts it at CHF 2,000–5,000 in most cases — [Helvetia](https://www.helvetia.com/ch/web/it/clienti-aziendali/consigli/pmi/costituire-una-sagl-in-svizzera.html)
- **Ditta individuale**: no notary is needed. Register fee CHF 0–120, total CHF 0–1,500 — [PostFinance](https://www.postfinance.ch/it/blog/blog-sul-business/quanto-costa-costituire-unimpresa.html). Registration is mandatory only above CHF 100,000 annual turnover, and the Ticino fee is about CHF 120 — [Magic Heidi (commercial source)](https://magicheidi.ch/it/sole-propietorship-or-llc)
- **Comparison of legal forms**: [KMU/SECO legal-form comparison table (2023)](https://www.kmu.admin.ch/dam/kmu/it/dokumente/savoir-pratique/formes-juridiques/rechtsformenvergleich-tabelle.pdf.download.pdf/ITAL_2023_Rechtsformenvergleich.pdf)
- **AVS/AI/IPG for the self-employed (2025)**:
  - 10.0% from CHF 60,500 of income upward.
  - A decreasing scale applies between CHF 10,100 and 60,500, starting at 5.371%.
  - Minimum contribution CHF 530 a year.
  - Source: [UFAS/BSV scale table](https://sozialversicherungen.admin.ch/it/d/6139/download); [AHV-IV changes 1.1.2025](https://shop.ahv-iv.ch/data/docs/de/75135/Revisioni-1%C2%B0-pilastro-1%C2%B0-gennaio-2025.pdf)
  - From 1 Jan 2027 the scale limits become CHF 10,300 and CHF 61,500 — [UFAS](https://sozialversicherungen.admin.ch/it/d/6954/download)
  - One source citing a 10.1% rate and a CHF 1,010 minimum appears outdated or conflicting.
- **Corporate tax**: TaxRep (updated Dec 2025) puts the total burden at about **18.6%** in ordinary cases after the reform — [TaxRep](https://taxrep.us/it/guida_fiscale/guida-fiscale-svizzera-per-le-imprese/imposta-cantonale-sul-capitale-delle-imprese/imposta-sul-capitale-sociale-in-ticino/rates/). The cantonal profit-tax rate was cut from 8% to 5.5% — [GC report 2025](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/POTERI/GC/allegati/rapporti/28238_8303%20R-magg.pdf). A 2021 SUPSI projection gave 18.9% nominal and 15.9% effective from 2025 — [laRegione](https://www.laregione.ch/cantone/ticino/1559089/servizi). The sources differ slightly, so verify with the Divisione delle contribuzioni.
- **Permits**: an EU/EFTA citizen who will be self-employed while living across the border gets a **G permit valid 5 years**. Activity can start once the application is filed with the Ufficio della migrazione. Proof that the activity is "effective and lasting" can be requested and the permit revoked if it is not. Health-sector work needs a separate authorisation — [ti.ch Ufficio migrazione leaflet (2017 version)](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DI/DI_DI/SEZPOP/documentazione/avvertenze/2017/G_Avvertenze_Cittadini_UE-AELS_indipendenti.pdf)
- **Wages and purchasing power**: the 2024 median gross monthly wage was **CHF 5,708 in Ticino**, against CHF 7,024 for Switzerland and CHF 7,502 for Zurich, and the gap is widening — [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Salari-in-crescita-ma-il-Ticino-sempre-fanalino-di-coda--3306851.html); [RSI national](https://www.rsi.ch/info/svizzera/Nel-2024-in-Svizzera-il-salario-mediano-era-di-7%E2%80%99024-franchi-lordi--3305177.html)
- **Health premiums**: they rose **+7.1% in Ticino in 2026**, the highest increase of any canton (Swiss average +4.4%), to an average of CHF 501.50 a month — [blue News](https://www.bluewin.ch/it/attualita/svizzera/premi-cassa-malati-2026-aumento-medio-del-4-4-in-ticino-7-1-2885256.html). For 2027 the increase is +3.7% (Swiss average +5%), giving an average of about CHF 519.90 a month, the highest in Switzerland. A typical family will spend about CHF 21,346 a year on premiums in 2027 — [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/s/4121422); [laRegione](https://www.laregione.ch/1953442) (the figures differ slightly between outlets, e.g. CHF 519.19)

### Inferences
- **Income a founder needs**: a resident adult should plan for at least about CHF 5,000–5,700 gross a month (around CHF 60–70k a year) to match the Ticino median. That figure has to carry about 10% AVS, about CHF 500+/month of health premium and 2nd/3rd-pillar savings. A cross-border founder living in Italy has much lower living costs, which is a structural cost advantage for frontalieri setting up in Ticino.
- **Choice of legal form**: a ditta individuale is the cheap way to test demand. A Sagl makes sense once liability risk exists (building trades, care) or for credibility with business clients.

### Gaps
- I found no official 2025-2026 tariff for the Ticino commercial register (Ufficio del registro di commercio, Biasca) or for notary fees.
- No official cost-of-living or rent benchmark was gathered (the USTAT rent index was not retrieved).
- I did not verify the current personal income-tax burden for a self-employed person in Lugano versus the valleys.
- Rules for non-EU founders (third-country nationals need a cantonal economic-interest assessment) were not researched.

## Q3. Sector gaps in Ticino (demand > supply, or poor service quality)

### Takeaway
The strongest documented gaps are:
- **Home care and elder support**, a fast-growing market that Ticino's private providers already dominate, but which now faces regulatory caps.
- **Skilled building trades**: tilers, painters, carpenters, masons.
- **Childcare**, especially 0-1 year olds and non-standard hours.
- **Hospitality staffing**.
- **Energy retrofit**: a CHF 95M cantonal incentive pot for 2025-2031, with the PV market maturing.
- **Tourism services in the shoulder and winter season**: record overnight stays, and the Ticino365 strategy to fill November-February.

Housing vacancy is falling (1.92%), which weakens the "empty flats" story except in the Mendrisiotto. Viticulture is under pressure, not in expansion.

### Cited Findings
**Tourism**
- **2025 overall**: almost 1.7M tourists and nearly 4.5M overnight stays (USTAT tourism chapter) — [USTAT 2025 PDF](https://m3.ti.ch/DFE/DR/USTAT/allegati/prodima/4710_turismo.pdf). Total overnight stays across all accommodation types were about 8.5–8.6M (+3.7%), "one of the best results of the last 15 years" — [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Ticino-85-milioni-di-pernottamenti-turistici-nel-2025--3549112.html). The definitions behind these figures differ; check which accommodation types each one includes.
- **Hotels 2025**: 2,510,079 nights (+3.7%) and 1,218,930 arrivals, the highest since 2008. Swiss guests account for about 60% of nights. US nights rose 11% on 2024 and about 50% on 2019; UK nights rose 14%, Gulf nights fell 7%. Hotel nights averaged about 2.49M a year over 2022-2025, the best four-year period since 2010 — [Ticino Welcome](https://ticinowelcome.ch/turismo-cibo-e-vino/turismo-e-viaggi/bilancio-turismo-ticinese-2025/)
- **Campsites and short rentals 2025**: campsites had about 1.04M nights (+4.5%). Short-term rentals, including Airbnb, had about 1.13M nights, flat on 2024. BAK Economics forecasts hotel nights +2.3% in 2026 and +4.3% in 2027 — [Ticino Welcome](https://ticinowelcome.ch/turismo-cibo-e-vino/turismo-e-viaggi/bilancio-turismo-ticinese-2025/)
- **Ticino365 strategy** (launched Oct 2025): it aims to make November–February attractive, through Winterland, Monte Tamaro opening on winter weekends and the Isole di Brissago being open in winter. An action plan with the regional tourism organisations (OTR) and local partners is being drafted, and the 2026 communication focus is "hidden gems" — [Ticino Welcome](https://ticinowelcome.ch/turismo-cibo-e-vino/turismo-e-viaggi/bilancio-turismo-ticinese-2025/)
- **Luganese hotel nights 2025**: 998,234 (+2.5%) — [Ticino Welcome Luganese](https://ticinowelcome.ch/turismo-cibo-e-vino/turismo-e-viaggi/turismo-nel-luganese-crescita-pernottamenti-2025/)
- **Winter 2025/26** was a Swiss record of 18.7M nights (Nov–Apr) — [laRegione](https://www.laregione.ch/cantone/ticino/1930826/pernottamenti-ticino-grigioni-crescita-stagione-record-forte)
- **Short-rental rules**: since 1 Feb 2022, anyone renting short-term accommodation in Ticino must obtain a registration number under the cantonal tourism law and display it in the listing — [Airbnb help, Ticino](https://mk.airbnb.com/help/article/1653?locale=it)

**Hospitality staffing**
- GastroTicino has reported a "quite critical" shortage, especially of cooks. Some venues added an unscheduled closing day so they could keep shifts compliant with the collective labour agreement — [RSI (2022)](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Locali-chiusure-per-garantire-i-turni--1795432.html); [RSI (2022)](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Ristoranti-e-hotel-a-corto-di-personale--1797538.html)
- In July 2024 GastroTicino reported revenue down 20–50% over six months and warned of closures around the turn of 2024/2025 — [tio.ch (2024)](https://www.tio.ch/ticino/attualita/1770050/situazione-ristorazione-affari-chiusure-ticino); [laRegione (2024)](https://www.laregione.ch/1770059)

**Real estate**
- On 1 Jun 2025 the vacancy rate was **1.92%**, the lowest in 8 years: 4,936 empty dwellings, 371 fewer than in 2024. About 80.6% of them are for rent and 19.4% for sale. The Mendrisiotto had the highest rate (3.88%) and the Locarnese the lowest (1.02%) — [USTAT 2025](https://www3.ti.ch/DFE/DR/USTAT/allegati/news/254739ns_2025-29.pdf)
- The Swiss national vacancy rate fell to 0.93%, a record low, in 2026 — [laRegione](https://www.laregione.ch/tio/ticino/1949301/servizi)

**Energy retrofit and solar**
- The cantonal energy-incentive decree for 2025-2031 has a CHF 95M credit, of which **CHF 40M is for "conversione energetica"**: replacing direct-electric or fossil heating and replacing old heat pumps. Applications must be filed *before* work starts. Decisions up to CHF 50k are taken by SPAAS — [cantonal decree (RL)](https://m3.ti.ch/CAN/RLeggi/public/index.php/raccolta-leggi/pdfatto/atto/10761); [lexfind](https://www.lexfind.ch/tolv/250067/it)
- Municipal top-ups such as Lugano's (CHF 500–1,200) apply only if the cantonal incentive is granted — [Pura example regulation](https://pura.ch/MM-no-792-Modifica-del-Regolamento-comunale-per-l-erogazione-di-incentivi-in-favore-dell-efficienza-energetica-delle-energie-rinnovabili-e-della-mobilita-sostenibile-3df6b100?i=1)
- **PV market**: 2023 was a record year, with 71 MW installed and 229 MW in total. In **2024 more than 2,800 new systems added about 75 MW**, fewer installations than in 2023 but about the same capacity, which SUPSI called a "turning point" — [USTAT/SUPSI 2025 energy report](https://www3.ti.ch/DFE/DR/USTAT/allegati/volume/ed_2025_11.pdf); [laRegione](https://www.laregione.ch/cantone/ticino/1549746/servizi)
- During the 2022 boom, AIL had 320 PV quotes by March 2022, against 450 in all of 2021, and waiting times were long. Subsidies were about 20–30% of system cost — [RSI (2022)](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Corsa-al-fotovoltaico-privato-in-Ticino--1804169.html)

**Elderly care and home assistance**
- **Market growth**: Swiss home-care (Spitex) providers delivered 25.6M care hours in 2024 (+10.2%), driven by commercial firms. **Ticino was the first canton where for-profit providers overtook non-profits in hours delivered** — [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/info/svizzera/Boom-delle-cure-a-domicilio-in-Svizzera--3269975.html)
- **Spending and provider growth**: home-care spending per insured person in Ticino grew +153% over a decade, against +85% in Switzerland. Private services and independent nurses roughly tripled, from 24 private Spitex in 2016 to more than 60, and from 210 independent nurses to more than 500.
- **Moratorium**: the Canton consulted on a moratorium on new authorisations, possibly from 2025 — [RSI moratorium](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Moratoria-per-le-cure-a-domicilio-in-consultazione--2163354.html); [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Sostegno-ma-con-poco-entusiasmo-alla-moratoria-sulle-cure-a-domicilio--2164496.html); [RSI "freno al mercato selvaggio"](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/%E2%80%9CUn-freno-al-mercato-selvaggio-delle-cure-a-domicilio%E2%80%9D--1983268.html)
- **Demographics**: the over-80 population in Ticino is projected to grow 50% by 2030 — [laRegione](https://www.laregione.ch/cantone/ticino/1554245/popolazione-anziani-ticino-domicilio-pianificazione-assistenza-casa)

**Childcare**
- The cantonal report "Conciliabilità famiglia e lavoro 2025-2028" was published on 11 Apr 2025 — [ti.ch report](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DSS/DASF/UFaG/Rapporto_Conciliabilita_famiglia_e_lavoro_2025-2028.pdf); [summary](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DSS/DASF/UFaG/Riassunto_rilevazione_bisogni_conciliabilita.pdf)
- **Where places are short**: in the Luganese and Bellinzonese for ages 0-3, and in the Luganese and Locarnese for school-age children. Waiting lists concern mainly babies, so the priority is places for 0-1 year olds. Nidi and micro-nidi grew from 55 to 74 since 2019. Opening hours do not fit evening or night shift workers — [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Famiglia-e-lavoro-gli-sforzi-del-Cantone-per-la-conciliabilit%C3%A0--2813707.html)
- An older estimate put the shortfall at 801 places to reach 33% coverage — [USTAT older](https://www3.ti.ch/DFE/DR/USTAT/allegati/articolo/206dss_2011-2_4.pdf) (about 2011, outdated)

**Building trades and artisans**
- Ticino is seeing an "emorragia" (drain) of apprentice masons — [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Apprendistato-mancano-muratori--2177319.html)
- Italian-speaking Switzerland lacks tilers, painters and carpenters, with only 7 apprentice tile-layers cited. Jobs pay above CHF 4,000 a month and are aimed at frontalieri — [ComoZero](https://comozero.it/attualita/allarme-piastrellisti-ticino-mancano-apprendisti-edilizia-artigianato-4000-franchi/)
- The shortage of skilled construction labour, relative to building volume, is projected to reach 16% by 2040 in the main trades — [Ticinonews](https://www.ticinonews.ch/svizzera/carenza-di-manodopera-senza-artigiani-qualificati-non-si-possono-portare-a-termine-i-progetti-379571)
- Nationally, the Adecco skills-shortage index for 2025 was about 22% lower than the previous year, close to pre-pandemic levels — [Adecco Group](https://www.adeccogroup.com/it-ch/il-futuro-del-lavoro/swiss-skills-shortage/swiss-skills-shortage-2025). The labour market is easing overall, but trade shortages persist.

**Agriculture and viticulture**
- **2025 harvest**: about 4.8M kg of grapes, of which 3.67M kg Merlot, at an average 20.74 °Brix. The base price was CHF 420 per quintal. The estimated value was about CHF 19.85M, down 8.3% on the previous year. Downy mildew, sour rot and botrytis were problems, and the Japanese beetle (Popillia) keeps spreading — [ti.ch Comunicato annata viticola 2025](https://www4.ti.ch/fileadmin/DFE/DE-SA/comunicati/allegati/20251127_Comunicato_annata_viticola_2025.pdf); [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Vendemmia-2025-un%E2%80%99annata-difficile-per-la-viticoltura-ticinese--3311234.html)
- **Farm succession**: Ticino farms face a "pass it on or close" situation — [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Aziende-agricole-passo-o-chiudo--1869331.html)

**Cybersecurity and digital services for SMEs**
- Nationally, only 2 in 5 Swiss SMEs feel well prepared against cyber attacks. Of 515 firms surveyed, 4% suffered an attack, 5% extortion and 4% lost money to fraudulent emails — [tio.ch](https://www.tio.ch/svizzera/economia/1879358/pmi-sicurezza-informatici-fiducia-continua)
- In Ticino, Alessandro Trivilini (SUPSI) said that cybersecurity "is seen more as a cost than an investment" and that attacks do hit Ticino firms — [tio.ch](https://www.tio.ch/ticino/attualita/1175184/attenzione-ai-cyber-attacchi-possono-colpire-chiunque) (date unclear)
- Data-protection law (nDSG/nLPD) adds compliance pressure on SMEs — [tio.ch](https://www.tio.ch/svizzera/attualita/1603578/pmi-dati-protezione-legge-aziende-it-essere-sicurezza-cybersicurezza)

### Inferences
- **Most promising for a small, low-capital founder**, given the documented demand:
  - *Home and elderly support that is not LAMal care* (companionship, household help, transport, coordination of family carers). This avoids the moratorium on nursing licences while riding the 50% growth in over-80s.
  - *Building-trade micro-firms* (tiling, painting, carpentry, small renovations), possibly bundled with the paperwork for energy-incentive applications.
  - *Energy-retrofit consulting / "pratica incentivi" services*. The CHF 40M conversion pot requires applications before work starts, which is a natural intermediary role.
  - *Winter/shoulder-season tourism experiences* aligned with Ticino365.
  - *Childcare with non-standard hours, or micro-nidi in the Luganese/Bellinzonese*.
- **Lower-conviction areas**:
  - *Short-rental management*: volume is flat (1.13M nights) and registration is required. It is a crowded, mature segment, although management quality could still differentiate.
  - *New vineyards*: the value of the 2025 harvest fell and disease pressure is rising.
- **Purchasing power**: the low local median wage (CHF 5,708) caps prices for consumer services. Business-to-business services, tourist-facing services and services for well-off retirees and expatriates are likely to have better margins than mass consumer services.

### Gaps

## Q4. Examples of recent successful small startups/businesses (2020-2026) and business-succession platforms

### Takeaway
Concrete, verifiable examples of small Ticino businesses that succeeded in 2020-2026 were hard to find with the tools available. The visible examples are tech or biotech spin-offs: InVirtuoLabs (EUR 2.85M round) and BIMINI Biotech (EUR 3M seed), listed by the USI Startup Centre without dates. Business succession is a large, under-served market: about 5,500 Ticino firms are facing it, and only about 25% have a defined plan. I found no evidence of how well Swiss succession marketplaces (Companymarket, Successio, KMU-Nachfolge) cover Ticino.

### Cited Findings
- USI Startup news lists InVirtuoLabs, which closed a first round of EUR 2.85M, and BIMINI Biotech, which raised a EUR 3M seed round. No dates are given — [USI Startup news](https://www.startup.usi.ch/it/notizie-eventi/notizie?page=3)
- Ticino recorded 20 startup financing rounds in 2025 (EY) — [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/various/almost-3-3-billion-invested-in-swiss-start-ups/90987711)
- **Succession in Ticino**:
  - The Camera di commercio (Cc-Ti) cites **about 5,500 Ticino firms already facing succession**.
  - A Gruppo Multi / Cc-Ti / BancaStato / family-business association survey found only about **25%** of sampled firms had defined how they will pass on the business.
  - Of firms with a 2-year horizon, 63% had not defined a succession, sale or liquidation; for a 3-5 year horizon, 70% had not.
  - Sources: [laRegione publiredazionale](https://www.laregione.ch/temablog/-publiredazionale/1666272/servizi); [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Aziende-e-passaggi-di-testimone-un-quadro-difficile--1804866.html)
- A 2022 estimate said 34% of Ticino firms must find a succession solution within 5 years. Nationally, about 93,000 Swiss SMEs (15.1%) had a succession issue in April 2022 (Dun & Bradstreet) — [blue News](https://www.bluewin.ch/it/attualita/borsa-economia/aziende-successione-una-sfida-la-tradizione-familiare-vacilla-li.2994965) (2022 data)
- SUPSI expects many successions to happen by sale to competitors or managers rather than within the family — [SUPSI DEASS](https://www.supsi.ch/documents/d/deass/240903_deass_cctg_aspetti-fiscali-della-successione-aziendale)
- Federal guidance on succession planning — [KMU portal](https://www.kmu.admin.ch/kmu/it/home/savoir-pratique/nachfolge-betriebseinstellung/transmission-de-l-entreprise/transmettre-une-entreprise/bien-planifier-la-succession.html)

### Inferences
- **Buying an existing small business** (trade, restaurant, shop, fiduciary, farm) is a realistic and under-served entry route in Ticino. Supply of firms is large (about 5,500 facing succession), most owners are unprepared, and there is an Italian-language intermediation gap. A matchmaking or advisory service in Italian for small succession deals could itself be a niche business. This is inferred from the gap in coverage; it was not verified.

### Gaps
- I did not find a list of successful small, non-tech Ticino businesses from 2020-2026. startupticker.ch was not reachable through search results and the source was rate-limited.
- No data was found on Companymarket, Successio or KMU-Nachfolge listings in Ticino, or on Italian-language coverage. The listing counts per canton would need a direct visit to companymarket.ch.
- The dates and current status of InVirtuoLabs and BIMINI Biotech were not verified.
