# Global consumer and business trends 2025-2026 that open new small-business opportunities, and location-independent models for a Ticino-based founder

Research date: 14 Oct 2026. Scope: global, with notes on transferability to Ticino, Switzerland and Italy. These topics were covered in the previous round and are deliberately left out here: AI receptionists for trades, AI-native services and roll-ups, succession and search funds, generic AI agencies, horizontal micro-SaaS.

Method note: about 40 web searches and fetches. Many figures come from search-engine summaries of the cited pages. Where I opened the page myself, it says "(page read)". Market-research forecasts (FMI, QYR, GII and similar) are paid-report teasers; their figures often conflict and should be treated as indicative only. The 2026 dates in this note are real: the documents were published in 2026.

## 1. Repair and refurbishment economy (EU Right to Repair Directive, Swiss repair trends, refurbished)

### Takeaway
The EU Right to Repair Directive (2024/1799) mainly obliges manufacturers. Member states had to transpose it by 31 July 2026, and Italy missed that date. It strengthens independent repairers on paper: consumers may choose their repairer, a manufacturer cannot refuse because a third party repaired the item before, spare parts must be sold at reasonable prices, and a repair under guarantee adds 12 months. Public repair bonuses (Austria, France) show demand rises sharply when the price gap closes. Austria's bonus drew 1.7-1.8 million vouchers, while France's was barely used at first. Switzerland has no right-to-repair law and no repair bonus. Repair there is held back by high labour and parts costs, so the gap is real but price-sensitive.

### Cited Findings
**EU directive: status and content**
- The transposition deadline was 31 July 2026. Ireland's regulations took effect that day. A repair.eu post of 30 July 2026 said only "a handful" of member states had notified full transposition — [ERP Ireland](https://erp-recycling.org/ie/news-and-events/2026/08/ireland-transposes-the-eu-right-to-repair-directive/); [repair.eu](https://repair.eu/news/the-right-to-repair-directive/)
- Obligations: manufacturers must repair covered products on request and may not block repair. Spare parts and tools must be available to repairers "at reasonable prices". Consumers can choose any repairer, and the manufacturer cannot refuse a repair because another repairer worked on the item before. A repair under guarantee adds 12 months to the guarantee. The EU repair platform must have its common interface by 31 July 2027 and be fully running by 1 Jan 2028 — [Claimlane](https://www.claimlane.com/resources/blog/eu-right-to-repair-ecommerce); [Fabrico](https://www.fabrico.io/blog/eu-right-to-repair-directive-manufacturers-guide/)
- **Italy:** the draft legislative decree (Atto del Governo n. 426, 4 articles, based on Law 36/2026) was approved in preliminary form on 2 July 2026 and sent to Parliament on 9 July. The committees had until 18 August 2026 to give their opinion. Italy missed the 31 July deadline, and entry into force was expected "not before the second half of August" — [Smartworld](https://www.smartworld.it/news/direttiva-ue-diritto-riparazione-italia.html); [Altroconsumo](https://www.altroconsumo.it/vita-privata-famiglia/servizi-e-contratti/opinioni/diritto-riparazione); [Sky TG24, 2 Aug 2026](https://tg24.sky.it/economia/2026/08/02/diritto-riparazione-come-funziona-italia); [Senato dossier](https://www.senato.it/service/PDF/PDFServer/BGT/1513713.pdf). Conflict: cosedicasa.com (31 Jul 2026) described the right as already in force — [cosedicasa](https://www.cosedicasa.com/news/diritto-alla-riparazione-degli-elettrodomestici-cosa-cambia-dal-31-luglio-2026). I could not confirm whether the final decree has since been published in the Gazzetta Ufficiale.
- Germany was expected to meet the deadline; final adoption not confirmed — [BUSE](https://buse.de/en/blog-en/commercial-en/right-to-repair/)

**Repair bonuses: evidence that subsidies unlock demand**
- Austria: about 1.7 million vouchers were redeemed after the 2022 launch ([Salzburg24](https://www.salzburg24.at/news/oesterreich/reparaturbonus-geht-in-die-naechste-runde-art-296846)). Falter reports 1.8 million vouchers and €130M of mostly EU recovery funds ([Falter](https://www.falter.at/zeitung/20250715/von-der-werkbank-in-die-insolvenz)). The bonus paused on 26 May 2025 when funds ran out — [Bundeskanzleramt](https://www.bundeskanzleramt.gv.at/eu-aufbauplan/aktuelles/reparaturbonus-pausiert-foerderaktion-seit-26-mai-2025-ausser-kraft.html)
- Austria's successor, the "Geräte-Retter-Prämie", covers 50% of repair cost up to €130, plus up to €30 for a cost estimate, for household electrical devices. About 2,280 partner businesses take part. 65,300 vouchers were issued and 59,000 redeemed between 12 Jan and 26 Mar (year not stated) — [vienna.at](https://www.vienna.at/geraete-retter-praemie-run-auf-neuen-reparaturbonus/10080038). €30M a year is earmarked in the 2026 budget planning — [The International](https://www.theinternational.at/austria-relaunches-repair-bonus-as-device-saver-bonus/)
- France (2023): the bonus was €10-45. Of €62M budgeted, only about €1M had been used by November 2023 — [SRF Kassensturz, 7 Nov 2023](https://www.srf.ch/sendungen/kassensturz-espresso/kassensturz/reparieren-statt-wegwerfen-huerden-fuer-reparaturen-schweiz-flop-frankreich-top) (page read)

**Switzerland**
- Switzerland discards about 130,000 t of electrical devices a year, and a BAFU study found about half still work. Repairs are often uneconomic because of labour and spare-parts costs. Software-locked parts (iPhone 14 example) hurt independent repairers. There is no Swiss right-to-repair law. Existing players include 200+ volunteer Repair Cafés, the Reparaturführer directory, the startup "Pretty Good" (a repair-partner network with free online estimates), and Bern's "Restwert" repair-and-resale pilot — [SRF Kassensturz (2023)](https://www.srf.ch/sendungen/kassensturz-espresso/kassensturz/reparieren-statt-wegwerfen-huerden-fuer-reparaturen-schweiz-flop-frankreich-top) (page read)
- Swiss Repair Café output (2019, latest detailed figure): 48 cafés took in 7,903 items and fully repaired 5,190, extrapolated to about 23,050 attempts a year across 140 cafés — [Horizont](https://www.horizont.net/schweiz/nachrichten/konsumntenschutzrepair-cafs-reparieren-sie-schon-oder-entsorgen-sie-noch-182104); "over 200" cafés today — [Küssnacht](https://www.kuessnacht.ch/public/upload/assets/5346/20251001%20Umwelttipp%20Reparieren%20statt%20Rabatte%20jagen.pdf?fp=1)
- Revendo (Swiss refurbished electronics) runs 13 locations in 4 countries with 140 employees. Physical stores bring 50% of revenue. Its products are also sold on Ricardo. It aimed for 1M devices a year by 2025; I found no evidence on whether that was met — [KMU Portal interview](https://www.kmu.admin.ch/kmu/en/home/new/interview/2023/sustainability-must-provide-a-tangible-benefit-to-consumers.html); [Horizont](https://www.horizont.net/schweiz/nachrichten/eine-partnerschaft-fuers-nachhaltige-leben-refurbished-elektronikgeraete-von-revendo-neu-auf-ricardo-195186); [Organisator](https://organisator.ch/en/?p=16905)
- E-bikes and bikes: in 2025, 316,050 bikes and e-bikes were delivered to Swiss retailers, down 7.4%, after a post-Covid consolidation since 2023. E-gravel bikes more than doubled to 2,162 units, gravel bikes rose a third to 21,702, and road bikes rose about 9% to 17,524 — [Swissinfo/SDA](https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/velo-verk%c3%a4ufe-in-der-schweiz-normalisieren-sich-nach-corona-boom/91087595). E-bikes were 45% of 2024 sales (341,142 units) — [Bluewin](https://www.bluewin.ch/en/news/almost-every-second-bike-sold-in-2024-was-an-e-bike-2606208.html)

### Inferences
- The directive helps manufacturers' authorised networks as much as independents. For a small player, the opening is less "repair shop under the new law" and more (a) a **multi-brand repair broker/aggregator** in Italian (cost estimates online, pick-up, network of artisans, like "Pretty Good"), (b) **e-bike battery and motor service**, where the large post-2020 e-bike fleet now ages out of warranty (an inference from 45% e-bike share; I have no workshop data), and (c) **refurbished niches** not served by Revendo or Back Market (pro audio, coffee machines, e-bikes, Garmin/Apple Watch).
- In Ticino, high labour costs make device repair hard to monetise. Repair works where item value is high: e-bikes (CHF 3-6k), coffee machines, premium audio, watches.
- Italy (Lombardy, Piedmont) is the bigger market once the decree is final, but low prices for labour make it margin-thin.

### Gaps
- No market size or revenue data for independent repair shops after the directive. No Swiss repair-bonus scheme was found (none appears to exist). Italy's final decree publication is unconfirmed as of mid-October 2026. No Back Market Switzerland figures. No e-bike workshop backlog data.

## 2. Second-hand / resale: reselling, consignment for busy people, pre-owned luxury and watches

### Takeaway
Resale keeps growing at about 3x the rate of firsthand fashion. Vinted hit €1.1bn revenue in 2025 (+38%) on €10.8bn GMV. In Switzerland, Ricardo says about 70% of items sold are used goods, and second-hand clothing sales rose 31% in two years. Platforms are removing friction (free Post pick-up, AI listing text), which weakens the "we list it for you" model for low-value goods. Consignment works for **high-value items** (luxury bags, watches, jewellery), where eBay + Linda's Stuff and Rebag take about 35% commission. Pre-owned watches are the Swiss-specific angle: 40% of Gen Z plan to buy one in the next 12 months.

### Cited Findings
- Vinted 2025: revenue about €1.1bn (+38%), net profit €62M (−19% because of investment in Germany, new categories and logistics). It added sporting goods and collectibles — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.com/news/business/vinted-reaches-1-1-billion-euros-in-revenue-for-2025-accepting-a-strategic-drop-in-profits/2026040971654). GMV €10.8bn (+47%) — [Irish Examiner](https://www.irishexaminer.com/business/companies/arid-41824514.html)
- BCG x Vestiaire Collective (Oct 2025): the second-hand fashion and luxury market is about $210-220bn and could reach up to $360bn by 2030. It grows about 10% a year, around 3x firsthand. Resale makes up 28% of surveyed users' wardrobes and 40% for handbags. Handbags and jewellery drive value — [BCG press release](https://www.bcg.com/press/9october2025-new-report-from-bcg-and-vestiaire-collective-reveals-global-trends-reshaping-the-resale-market)
- Ricardo (CH): about 69% of listings and 70% of items sold in 2025 were second-hand. Zurich, Bern and Aargau supply 45% of second-hand listings, and Schwyz leads per capita — [SMG](https://swissmarketplace.group/media-release/secondhand-champions-these-cantons-are-driving-the-circular-economy/). In 2024, 600,000+ second-hand clothing items were sold on Ricardo, +31% in two years — [Bluewin](https://www.bluewin.ch/en/news/trade-in-used-clothing-on-the-rise-in-switzerland-li.2682715). After Christmas 2025, 302,239 second-hand products were listed in 10 days (+35%) — [SMG](https://swissmarketplace.group/media-release/ricardo-sees-post-christmas-boom-in-gift-resales/)
- Platform-side friction removal: Ricardo has offered free Swiss Post pick@home for sellers since May 2025 (normally CHF 4.50) — [SMG](https://swissmarketplace.group/media-release/ricardo-free-pickhome-by-swiss-post/). "Ricardo AI" writes listings from a photo — [Blick](https://www.blick.ch/wirtschaft/swiss-marketplace-chef-christoph-tonini-ueber-die-wichtigsten-schweizer-online-marktplaetze-wir-stehen-im-harten-wettbewerb-mit-giganten-id20326320.html)
- Old but relevant (2016 Tutti/LINK survey): an estimated CHF 4.2bn of unused goods "dormant" in Swiss households (a stock, not annual sales) — [SMG](https://swissmarketplace.group/media-release/second-hand-market-survey-2021/)
- Swiss Post publishes a "Re-commerce study 2026" (1,000 respondents); its findings were not retrieved — [Post](https://www.post.ch/en/business-solutions/circular-economy/re-commerce-studie)
- Consignment model: eBay with partner Linda's Stuff handles photography, pricing and listing, and pays the consignor a percentage on sale. It started with bags and in 2024 extended to fine jewellery, watches and footwear. Rebag's consignment rate was around 35% (2023) — [Glossy](https://www.glossy.co/fashion/luxury-resale-platforms-are-relying-on-consignment-to-bring-in-more-supply/); [RetailTouchPoints](https://www.retailtouchpoints.com/topics/digital-commerce/e-commerce-experience/ebay-expands-consignment-service-to-jewelry-watches-and-footwear). US luxury resale is estimated at $8.65bn in 2024, rising to $13.04bn by 2030 (market estimate) — [RetailBoss](https://retailboss.co/us-luxury-resale-market-grow-8-billion-to-13-billion-dollars-by-2030-as-sellers-weigh-consignment-vs-direct-sales/)
- Watches (Deloitte Swiss Watch Industry Study 2025): 40% of Gen Z plan to buy a pre-owned watch in the next 12 months, versus 20% of Boomers. The main reasons are affordability (53%) and access to discontinued or unique models (36%). 38% prefer multi-brand stores. A 39% US tariff on Swiss watches has applied since Aug 2025 — [Deloitte CH press release](https://www.deloitte.com/ch/en/about/press-room/swiss-watch-industry-study-2025.html) (page read). A claim that the secondary market will pass CHF 35bn in 2026 appears only in a blog, not verified in Deloitte material — [TickingTales](https://tickingtales.com/swiss-watch-pressure-and-potential-inside-deloitte-2025-report/)

### Inferences
- **"We sell it for you" for ordinary household goods** is a weak business in CH: platforms now give free pick-up and AI listings, and the labour cost per item is high. It is viable only as (a) **estate and downsizing clearance (Räumung/sgombero) plus resale**, linked to the aging theme (Ticino has Switzerland's oldest population, see §3), or (b) **high-ticket consignment** (watches, bags, jewellery, designer furniture, e-bikes), with a 20-35% commission.
- **Pre-owned watch sourcing, authentication and resale** from Ticino fits local know-how and the cross-border Italian buyer base. Saturation is high among large dealers (Chrono24 ecosystem). A niche would need specialisation (vintage Swiss mid-tier, women's watches, Gen Z entry pieces), and trust or authentication would be the differentiator.

### Gaps
- No revenue data for small consignment businesses in Europe. No independent authentication-service economics. No Swiss total second-hand market value for 2025.

## 3. Silver economy (B2C beyond care): digital help for seniors, home adaptation, downsizing

### Takeaway
Ticino is Switzerland's oldest canton: 23.7% of residents were 65+ at end-2023, projected at 32.3% by 2050. In 2025, people over 65 outnumbered under-20s nationwide for the first time. Most Swiss over-65s are now online (9 in 10), so the need is moving from "learn to use the internet" toward **ongoing tech concierge, AI/scam protection, and device setup**. Home adaptation (bathroom, stairlift) has clear price points (about €8k for a bathroom, €3k+ for a stairlift) but weak Swiss subsidy support.

### Cited Findings
- Ticino: 23.7% aged 65+ at end-2023, 32.3% in 2050 (medium scenario) — [USTAT](https://m3.ti.ch/DFE/DR/USTAT/allegati/volume/d_16_documento.pdf); [laRegione](https://www.laregione.ch/1834480). Ticino ranks ahead of Graubünden (23.6%) and Basel-Landschaft (23.3%) — [tio.ch](https://www.tio.ch/ticino/attualita/1939418/dove-l-eta-avanza-piu-in-fretta-il-ticino-laboratorio-della-svizzera-che-sara). Switzerland 2025 (provisional): 1,811,000 people over 65 vs 1,802,000 under 20 — [tio.ch](https://www.tio.ch/svizzera/attualita/1915638/il-dato-che-preoccupa-gli-over-65-superano-i-minori-di-20-anni-svizzera-troppo-vecchia)
- Pro Senectute "Digital Seniors 2025": 9 in 10 people over 65 use the internet, more than double 2010. 88% of 65-74 year olds have basic digital skills, but over-85s much less. For the first time more older people get information via smartphones, tablets or PCs (33%) than via TV (30%). Pro Senectute now offers AI courses alongside beginner courses — [Pro Senectute press release 4 Jun 2025](https://be.prosenectute.ch/assets/bilder-flyer-pdf/news/2025/20250604_medienmitteilung_digital_seniors_2025.pdf)
- Global senior tech services market: about $4.6bn in 2025, $13.75bn by 2035 (11.5% CAGR). Named players: Geek Squad, Papa, GetSetUp, AARP Tech Help — [Future Market Insights](https://www.futuremarketinsights.com/reports/senior-tech-services-market) (paid-report teaser). The B2B2C model of a tech concierge with an onsite help desk in senior living (The Smarter Service, 2025) — [McKnight's](https://www.qgdigitalpublishing.com/article/Demand+for+tech+help+leads+to+new+concierge+services/4936148/841503/article.html)
- Home adaptation costs (Germany): a bathroom conversion averages €7,949 (KfW evaluation). Stairlifts start at €3,000, platform lifts €8-15k, home elevators €20-50k+. German subsidy KfW 455 gives up to €6,250 plus care insurance up to €4,000 — [Aroundhome](https://www.aroundhome.de/altersgerechtes-wohnen/altersgerechter-umbau-kosten/). In Switzerland about 24 cantons need no permit for stairlifts, and health insurers contribute only partially — [Zurich parliament doc](https://parlzhcdws.cmicloud.ch/parlzh5/cdws//Files/0a8812c29a4045da9b9e071663929062-332/1/pdf); [HelveticCare](https://www.helveticcare.ch/treppenlift-alternative)

### Inferences
- In Ticino, a **"tecnico di fiducia per anziani"** subscription (setup, scams, e-banking, photos, AI assistants) sold to adult children (often living elsewhere in CH or abroad) and to residenze/case anziani is plausible. Pro Senectute is the free or low-cost incumbent for courses, so a commercial offer must be home visits plus remote support, not classes.
- **Downsizing and estate services** (house clearance, sale of contents via Ricardo/consignment, move to apartment, home adaptation project management) combine §2 and §3 and fit an aging canton with high property values. This is an inference; I found no data on demand or prices for this.

### Gaps
- No Swiss price points for senior tech help. No Swiss data on bathroom or stairlift demand. No for-profit franchise revenue (FDD Item 19) found for senior tech help. Senior travel was not researched for lack of time.

## 4. Wellness, loneliness/community, GLP-1 economy, longevity

### Takeaway
The wellness economy reached $6.8tn in 2024 (+7.9%), with Europe at $1.7tn. Social sauna is the clearest new format: the UK went from 45 Finnish-style saunas in 2023 to 100+ in 2024 and an expected 200+ by end-2025, sold as an alcohol-free way to socialise. GLP-1 drugs are changing food habits: users eat out less and snack less. In Switzerland about 40,000 people had Wegovy reimbursed in 2024, with fast growth in 2025. That creates demand for **protein-focused nutrition, muscle-preserving strength training and coaching** around the drug.

### Cited Findings
- GWI Global Wellness Economy Monitor 2025: $6.8tn in 2024, +7.9% vs 2023, 6.12% of global GDP. North America $2.3tn, Asia-Pacific $2.0tn, Europe $1.7tn. Europe is among the fastest-growing regions — [GWI](https://globalwellnessinstitute.org/?p=1238); [Health Club Management](https://www.healthclubmanagement.co.uk/health-club-management-news/EMBARGOED-GWI-report-global-wellness-economy-will-be-worth-nearly-US7trn-by-2025/348875). Forecast of about $9.8tn by 2029 (7.6%/yr) via a secondary source — [Sauna from Finland](https://saunafromfinland.com/news/global-wellness-economy-growing-at-record-pace-a-bright-future-for-the-finnish-sauna/). The Global Wellness Summit 2025 trends report includes a "Sauna Reimagined" chapter — [Spa Business](https://www.spabusiness.com/wellness-news/New-Global-Wellness-Summit-report-forecasts-top-10-wellness-trends-for-2025/354952)
- UK social sauna: the British Sauna Society counted 45 saunas in 2023, 100+ in 2024 and expects 200+ by end-2025. Saunas are used to socialise without alcohol — [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/66565/1/why-saunas-are-the-hottest-place-to-hang-out-right-now-peckham-london-uk); Community Sauna Baths network expanding in London — [Country & Town House](https://www.countryandtownhouse.com/style/health-and-beauty/community-saunas-trend/)
- GLP-1 consumer impact (Morgan Stanley AlphaWise survey of about 300 US users): 63% spend less at restaurants, 61% less on takeout and delivery. 68% eat fewer meals or snacks and 44% eat smaller portions. 71% eat less ice cream — [Green Queen](https://www.greenqueen.com.hk/glp-1-restaurants-food-survey-weight-loss-drugs-ozempic/); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/04/22/charted-changing-food-habits-closer). Grocery spend falls 6-9% after starting — [TipRanks/The Fly](https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/glp-1-users-grocery-spending-declines-6-9-after-starting-says-morgan-stanley). Morgan Stanley expects a $105bn drug market by 2030 — [Green Queen](https://www.greenqueen.com.hk/glp-1-restaurants-food-survey-weight-loss-drugs-ozempic/). Counter-signal: Lindt says GLP-1 users eat *more* premium chocolate — [News24](https://www.news24.com/business/companies/lindt-says-ozempic-other-glp-1-users-eat-more-chocolate-not-less-20260310-0731)
- KPMG: GLP-1 is prescribed alongside lifestyle changes (nutrition, exercise), which creates openings for adjacent services — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2024/glp-1-meds-impact-on-food-and-bev-ind.pdf)
- Switzerland: Wegovy has been reimbursed since March 2024. About 40,000 patients in 2024 cost CHF 43M (insurer association figure), and insurers spent half the 2024 total in Q1 2025 alone — [LeNews, Jul 2025](https://lenews.ch/2025/07/04/swiss-insurers-fear-cost-explosion-from-weight-loss-drugs/). Conflict: Deloitte puts 2024 spend at about CHF 5.8M — [Deloitte CH](https://www.deloitte.com/ch/en/Industries/financial-services/blogs/weight-loss-medications.html). Women use it more (6.9 vs 2.8 daily doses per 1,000), and ages 41-60 most — [LeNews, Jun 2025](https://lenews.ch/2025/06/06/women-more-likely-to-take-obesity-medication-in-switzerland/). Reimbursement requires a prescription from an endocrinologist or obesity centre, and Mounjaro is not reimbursed — [Comparis](https://en.comparis.ch/krankenkassen/leistungen/abnehmspritze). Swissmedic warned (Aug 2025) about falsified GLP-1 products — [Swissinfo](https://swissinfo.ch/eng/multinational-companies/can-new-fat-loss-drugs-help-switzerland-fight-rising-obesity-levels/79134632)

### Inferences
- **Social sauna on a lake** (Lugano and Lake Maggiore lidos, mobile wood-fired sauna) is a credible Ticino format with low capex compared with padel. It needs public-land or lido concessions. Nordic sauna culture already exists in German-speaking CH, so Ticino is less served (inference; I found no Ticino count).
- **GLP-1 companion programmes** (strength training to keep muscle, protein meal plans, a "what to eat on Wegovy" Italian-language online programme) could be sold B2C online across CH and Italy. The regulatory boundary is that it must not give medical advice, so partnering with obesity centres or endocrinologists is the channel.

### Gaps
- No revenue, pricing or unit economics for sauna, cold plunge or recovery studios. No Swiss or Italian count of social saunas. No size for the GLP-1 coaching market. Longevity clinics were not researched.

## 5. Pet humanisation economy

### Takeaway
The pet market is large and steady but slow-growing (Italy +2.5% in 2025 to €5.3bn; Swiss pet food about €0.68bn, +2%/yr). Online is a minority channel (11.5% in Italy). Cats grow fastest. Swiss pet ownership rose strongly, with cat registrations up about 150% from 2016 to 2024. Opportunities are in **services** (sitting, training, grooming, vet tele-advice, pet-friendly travel) rather than food, which is dominated by large brands and retailers.

### Cited Findings
- Italy (Assalco-Zoomark Report 2026, 2025 data): pet food plus pet care €5.3bn omnichannel (+2.5%). Dog and cat food is €4.2bn (79%, +2.7% value, 811k t) and pet care €1.1bn. 2022-2025 average value growth was 6.9%. Online is 11.5% (€483M). Specialist channel €1.7bn (+3.6%). Cats lead (+4.3%) — [QuiFinanza](https://quifinanza.it/lifestyle/pet-economy/animali-domestici-spesa-italia-2025/996227/amp/); [Zoomark PetTrend Jun 2026](https://www.zoomark.it/media/zoomark/diconodinoi/2026/2606_-_PetTrend_giugno.pdf). A broader pet-economy estimate is €6.8-7bn — [Milano Finanza](https://www.milanofinanza.it/news/un-tesoro-nella-cuccia-2665541)
- Switzerland: almost 1 in 2 households has a pet. Registered cats rose about 150% and dogs 13%+ between 2016 and 2024 (Identitas) — [Swissquote](https://www.swissquote.com/en-ch/splendid-pet-market). Swiss dog care market $172.3M (2024, 4.1% CAGR 2019-24) — [Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/5992669/dog-care-in-switzerland). Pet food €0.68bn in 2025, 2.14% CAGR — [Statista](https://fr.statista.com/outlook/cmo/food/pet-food/switzerland)

### Inferences
- Product businesses (premium food, DTC) face low growth and incumbents. Better small-business fits in Ticino: **pet-friendly tourism curation** (Ticino as a dog-friendly destination for German-Swiss tourists), **cat-sitting and boarding** (cats are the growth segment), and **Italian-language vet tele-triage** in partnership with vets.
