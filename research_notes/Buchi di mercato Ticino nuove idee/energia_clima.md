# Energy communities, energy transition and climate adaptation: business openings in Ticino and Northern Italy (as of Oct 2026)

Scope note: this does NOT cover the "cantonal heating-replacement incentive paperwork service" already recommended in an earlier round. Research date: 14 Oct 2026. About 20 search/fetch calls. Several sources are vendor marketing pages; they are flagged.

## 1. Switzerland: LEG/CLE (local electricity communities) and RCP/ZEV/vZEV

### Takeaway
LEGs (Italian: CLE/CEL) became legal on 1 Jan 2026. Swissolar estimates about 1,860 were set up in H1 2026, with about 7.2 participants each, and very uneven regional uptake. Ticino utilities (AIL, AET, Repower) publish forms and rules but no service fees. Billing is mostly sold as German-language SaaS (PVshare about CHF 30/participant/year; Upgrid about CHF 50–100/consumer/year). That leaves room for an Italian-language "setup + internal regulation + representative + billing" service for small LEGs, RCPs and vRCPs in Ticino.

### Cited Findings
- About 1,860 LEGs in Switzerland by end of H1 2026, extrapolated from a Swissolar survey of the 30 largest grid operators plus LEGhub data. The survey counted 1,323 LEGs in areas covering about 71% of the population. Average about 7.2 participants (regional averages range from 3 to 29). Participants: 78% pure consumers, 9% pure producers, 13% prosumers. Swissolar reports "strong demand" but spread "anything but even": some regions have none. Causes cited: demand, differing processes, IT systems and grid-operator offers. The article's intro says "just under 2,000", which is inconsistent with 1,860. — [Solarserver, 2 Sep 2026](https://www.solarserver.de/2026/09/02/schweiz-gruendet-1-860-lokale-stromgemeinschaften-leg-im-ersten-halbjahr-2026); also [swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/ger/hochrechnung:-mehr-als-1800-lokale-stromgemeinschaften-gegr%C3%BCndet/91990377)
- No canton or Ticino breakdown is published in that article. — [Solarserver](https://www.solarserver.de/2026/09/02/schweiz-gruendet-1-860-lokale-stromgemeinschaften-leg-im-ersten-halbjahr-2026)
- AIL (Lugano) CLE rules:
  - Members must be on the same grid operator and in the same Comune.
  - Generation must be at least 5% of the members' total contracted power.
  - Consumers without PV can join.
  - Private meters are not allowed; the grid operator's smart meters must be used.
  - The grid operator supplies official data, and the CLE "representative" processes it.
  - Downloadable forms: a preliminary information form and a CLE notification form.
  - AIL does not publish fees or CLE counts, and there are "no final requirements" on internal pricing.
  - AIL positions itself as promoter and manager of its own CLEs.
  — [AIL CLE page](https://www.ail.ch/privati/elettricita/prodotti/comunita-solari/CLE.html); [AIL comuni/comunità solari](https://www.ail.ch/comuni/elettricita/prodotti/comunita-solari.html)
- Repower (active in Ticino/Grisons) states the same conditions in Italian: same municipality, same distribution area, at least 5% own production. — [Repower CEL](https://www.repower.com/ch/it/solare/informazioni-generali/comunita-elettrica-locale)
- AET: from 2026, plants that received the one-off FER contribution may join a CLE. — [AET FER](https://aet.ch/fer); [Ticino Welcome on updated FER regulation 2026](https://ticinowelcome.ch/banche-e-imprese/industria-e-artigianato/comunita-locali-energia-elettrica-regolamento-fer-2026/)
- Plants paid under the old RIC-TI feed-in remuneration may join an RCP but are excluded from CLEs. — [MoneyMag.ch](https://www.moneymag.ch/Pannelli-solari-quasi-obbligatori)
- Grid operators have 3 months to set up registered CLEs. First meaningful data was expected "from summer 2026". — [Strom.ch / elettricitalocale.ch](https://www.strom.ch/it/notizie-e-media/elettricitalocalech-nuovi-strumenti-le-cle-rcpv-e-rcp)
- Network tariff discount on locally shared power: 40% when only one network level is used (same transformer circuit), 20% when two levels are used (same substation). Cited legal basis is StromVV Art. 19h; Swissolar lobbies for 60%. Smart meters must be installed within 3 months of a request.
  - Upgrid's estimated savings: about CHF 134/year for a typical consumer (~11%), and +CHF 325/year for a 10 kWp producer versus feed-in.
  - Upgrid's estimated Swiss median saving: about CHF 174/year.
  - These are vendor estimates and internally inconsistent: a calculator example on the same page shows CHF 521/year.
  - About 590 grid operators in Switzerland.
  — [Upgrid LEG guide (vendor)](https://upgrid.ch/de/articles/leg-schweiz-2026); [EKZ FAQ](https://www.ekz.ch/de/blue/wissen/2025/LEG-wichtigste-Fragen-kompakt-beantwortet.html)
- Billing price points:
  - PVshare: CHF 30.25/participant/year excl. VAT for vZEV/LEG billing (Pro+), plus CHF 9.70/year per solar plant, with a 3-year minimum contract. — [PVshare costs](https://pvshare.ch/de/kosten)
  - Upgrid: free for founders and consumers apart from about CHF 50–100/year admin per consumer, plus a per-kWh margin. — [Upgrid](https://upgrid.ch/de/articles/leg-schweiz-2026)
  - LEG Manager: pay-as-you-go or subscription, no public prices. — [LEG Manager](https://leg-manager.ch/)
  - Other tools: [LEG App](https://leg-app.ch/), [LEG price sheets](https://leg-app.ch/preisblaetter)
  - Some utilities do the billing themselves and carry the collection risk (e.g., Regio Energie Solothurn). — [Regio Energie](https://www.regioenergie.ch/de/leg-/)
- RCP (ZEV):
  - Members share one grid connection and are one customer for the grid operator.
  - Internal billing is "often with the support of a billing service provider".
  - Production must be at least 10% of connected power.
  - In landlord–tenant RCPs, the price must be between 0% and 80% of the utility's equivalent product (LAEl).
  - Virtual RCP (vRCP) has been possible since 1 Jan 2025.
  — [TicinoEnergia: instaurare un RCP](https://www.ticinoenergia.ch/it/blog/instaurare-un-rcp.html); [Helion RCP guide](https://www.helion.ch/it/guide/fotovoltaico/rcp/); [Repower RCP/RCPv](https://www.repower.com/ch/it/solare/informazioni-generali/raggruppamento-ai-fini-del-consumo-proprio-rcp-e-rcp-virtuale)
- AMB (Bellinzona) FAQ lists the cost items of a traditional RCP:
  - metering points (physical and virtual), billed by the chosen service provider;
  - internal management and billing, billed by the chosen service provider;
  - modification of private electrical installations, paid by participants.
  AMB itself offers RCP services. — [AMB FAQ RCP (PDF)](https://www.amb.ch/media/fh0i4yxh/faq-rcp.pdf); [AMB RCP service](https://www.amb.ch/privati/servizi-energetici/rcp/)
- Italian-language providers or info sources found: AIL, AET, AMB, Repower, Helion (Helion One "tariffa vicinato"), NeoVac (metering/billing), Ingene, TicinoEnergia (neutral advice), and lokalerstrom.ch (neutral DE/FR/IT platform run by VSE + Swissolar, funded by EnergieSchweiz). — [Helion tariffa vicinato](https://www.helion.ch/it/helion-one/vendita-corrente-solare/tariffa-vicinato/); [NeoVac RCP](https://www.neovac.ch/it/conoscenza/rcp); [lokalerstrom.ch RCP IT](https://www.lokalerstrom.ch/it/modelli-operativi/rcp); [Ingene](https://ingene.ch/it/2024/05/31/raggruppamento-ai-fini-del-consumo-proprio-cose-e-suoi-vantaggi/); [Città dell'energia: RCP, RCPv e CLE](https://www.energiestadt.ch/it/tipps/costituire-comunita-di-consumo-rcp-rcpv-e-cle-15089.html); [SvizzeraEnergia story](https://www.svizzeraenergia.ch/stories/comunita-elettriche/)
- Swissolar says "many companies" offer complete ZEV billing solutions. There is no public count of ZEV/vZEV in Switzerland. — [Swissolar ZEV page](https://www.swissolar.ch/de/wissen/wirtschaftlichkeit/zev-eigenverbrauch); [Swissolar HER handbook 2025](https://www.swissolar.ch/01_wissen/wirtschaftlichkeit/zev/handbuch-eigenverbrauchsregelung-her-2025.pdf)

### Inferences
- With an average of about 7 participants and consumer savings of roughly CHF 100–300/year, a LEG cannot pay much for administration. A solo founder cannot make money per LEG on software. The viable model is a done-for-you Italian-language service:
  - one-off setup fee: partner matching, internal regulation/statutes, utility forms, choice of discount level;
  - plus acting as the "rappresentante", or reselling a SaaS (white-label PVshare/LEG App) with Italian support.
  - Rough unit economics (inference, not sourced): a setup fee of a few hundred CHF and about CHF 30–60/participant/year.
- Better targets are RCP/vRCP in PPE condominiums and rental buildings. There, landlords have a legal mechanism to sell solar power to tenants at up to 80% of the utility price, and the per-building value is larger.
- AIL positions itself as manager of its own CLEs. Competing head-on with AIL in Lugano is risky. Smaller grid areas (AMB, SES, AGE, municipal utilities) and municipalities, particularly "Città dell'energia" communes, are more likely to buy or partner.
- Ticino-specific friction: RIC-TI plants are excluded from CLEs; FER one-off contribution plants are allowed. Explaining "which of my plants can join what" is an advisory product in itself.

### Gaps
- No Ticino or canton count of CLEs, and no official BFE/ElCom count of ZEV/vZEV.
- No published fees from AIL, AET, SES or AGE for CLE/RCP management. SES and AGE CLE pages were not checked.
- The Italian-language availability of German-Swiss SaaS (PVshare, LEG Manager, Upgrid) was not verified.

## 2. Italy: CER (renewable energy communities) and CACER configurations

### Takeaway
Italy had 6,161 self-consumption configurations in operation at 31 Aug 2026 (about 62,000 withdrawal points, 523.6 MW), only about 10% of the 5 GW incentive quota. The PNRR 40% grant window closed on 30 Nov 2025, but about €1.0 bn was granted to 38,390 applications (2.3 GW) that still have to be built and connected. Tariff access currently ends on 31 Dec 2027, and the industry is asking to extend it to 2030. The bottleneck is administrative: complex GSE procedures, adding members and plants, and finding consumers. Management fees for small CERs are around €1,500–4,000/year (one unverified blog source).

### Cited Findings
- GSE data at 31 Aug 2026: 6,161 configurations in operation, about 62,000 withdrawal points, 523.6 MW (up from 174.5 MW at end 2025), just over 10% of the 5 GW quota under the CER Decree. — [Edilportale, Sep 2026](https://www.edilportale.com/news/2026/09/risparmio-energetico/incentivi-cer-chiesta-la-proroga-dell-accesso-al-2030_112061_27.html)
- Tariff deadline (DM 414/2023): the tariff is paid for 20 years. Access is open until the 30th day after 5 GW is reached, and no later than 31 Dec 2027. Ten associations asked the Minister (MASE) to extend access to 31 Dec 2030, or until 5 GW is reached, with a decision by end-2026. As of Sep 2026 this was not yet decided. — [Edilportale](https://www.edilportale.com/news/2026/09/risparmio-energetico/incentivi-cer-chiesta-la-proroga-dell-accesso-al-2030_112061_27.html); [PGcasa](https://www.pgcasa.it/articoli/risparmio-energetico-e-fotovoltaico/comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili-proroga-incentivi__50208)
- PNRR grant of up to 40% for municipalities under 50,000 inhabitants: the application window closed on 30 Nov 2025.
  - The Facility CACER budget rose from €795.5 m to €1,041.5 m (the +€246 m top-up).
  - The GSE granted about €1.014 bn to 38,390 applications, 2,300.7 MW. These are admitted, not necessarily operational.
  — [Edilportale](https://www.edilportale.com/news/2026/09/risparmio-energetico/incentivi-cer-chiesta-la-proroga-dell-accesso-al-2030_112061_27.html); [Rinnovabili.it on new GSE PNRR rules](https://www.rinnovabili.it/energia/comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili/contributi-pnrr-cacer-nuove-regole-gse/)
- Funded projects had to sign grant agreements by 30 Jun 2026. One source says works were due by 30 Jun 2026 with entry into operation by 31 Dec 2027; this was not verified on gse.it. — [search summary of Infobuild / Rinnovabili](https://www.infobuild.it/approfondimenti/decreto-pnrr-2026-cosa-cambia-per-energia-ed-edilizia/)
- An older Gruppo Era article speculated about an extension to April 2026 and operation to 2028; this is unconfirmed. — [Gruppo Era](https://gruppo-era.it/comunita-energetiche-rinnovabili-e-fondi-pnrr-tutto-quello-che-ce-da-sapere-su-proroghe-scadenze-e-opportunita-fino-al-2028/)
- Pain points cited by the associations:
  - complex procedures and slow processing;
  - changing operational rules;
  - difficulty adding new plants or members to existing configurations;
  - consumers must be found before energy can be shared.
  The European Court of Auditors (Special Report 10/2026) found Italian information "complete but often needs expert interpretation". — [Edilportale](https://www.edilportale.com/news/2026/09/risparmio-energetico/incentivi-cer-chiesta-la-proroga-dell-accesso-al-2030_112061_27.html)
- Regional:
  - Euricse counted 597 CERs registered with the GSE at 30 Sep 2025: Lombardia 98, Piemonte 78, the top regions.
  - RSE/PoliMi found Piemonte had the most initiatives (28) in H1 2024.
  — [search summary citing Vita.it / Euricse](https://www.vita.it/ecco-litalia-delle-comunita-energetiche/); [Osservatorio CER Piemonte](https://www.osservatoriocer.it/cer-piemonte/)
- Lombardia:
  - ARIA's regional monitoring system for self-consumption configurations has run since March 2026. CERs must report operating data by 15 July of the following year (a new compliance task).
  - Region Lombardia funding of about €47.7 m (FESR 2021-27): "Manifestazione di interesse CER Fase 2" (€20 m) and the "ReLoad CER" call for municipalities over 5,000 inhabitants.
  — [AMAT Sportello Energia, Apr 2026](https://sportelloenergia.amat-mi.it/2026/04/16/cer-in-lombardia-attivo-il-sistema-regionale-di-monitoraggio/); [Strategie Amministrative](https://www.strategieamministrative.it/dettaglio-news/2025114138-da-regione-lombardia-477-milioni-a-sostegno-delle-cer/); [ARIA energialombardia](https://www.energialombardia.eu/strumenti_incentivazione/manifestazione-di-interesse-cer)
- Players and pricing:
  - Sorgenia "CER Energia Italia": free membership, no fees, no lock-in. It claims the incentive is worth about one monthly bill per year.
  - Management packages from Enel X, Edison, Sorgenia and local ESCos: about €1,500–4,000/year for small-to-medium CERs (blog source, unverified).
  - Some CERs keep a share of the incentive to cover costs; the share depends on the statute.
  — [Sorgenia CER](https://www.sorgenia.it/partnership-rec-le-comunita-energetiche); [Sorgenia guide PDF](https://www.sorgenia.it/shared/files/editor/2_guida_cer_pmi_target_business_0.pdf); [Solare Industriale blog](https://www.solareindustriale.it/blog-fotovoltaico/come-creare-una-cer)
- The "referente" files the GSE application, uploads documents, reports changes in membership, and receives and redistributes the incentive. — [Solare Industriale](https://www.solareindustriale.it/blog-fotovoltaico/come-creare-una-cer); [GSE Regole operative CACER](https://www.gse.it/documenti_site/Documenti%20GSE/Servizi%20per%20te/AUTOCONSUMO/Gruppi%20di%20autoconsumatori%20e%20comunita%20di%20energia%20rinnovabile/Regole%20e%20procedure/ALLEGATO%201%20Regole%20Operative%20CACER.pdf)
- RSE publishes an interactive CER map. — [Lumi4innovation](https://www.lumi4innovation.it/mappa-cer-italia/)

### Inferences
- The opening is no longer "set up a CER". Big utilities offer free membership and the PNRR window has closed. The opening is operating the backlog:
  - about 38,000 PNRR-funded plants must reach operation by the deadlines;
  - thousands of small CERs need someone to add members, keep GSE records up to date, redistribute incentives, and file Lombardia's ARIA monitoring by 15 July.
- Customers are municipalities, parishes and associations that set up small CERs and lack staff. Possible formats: a "referente/back-office" service or a member-acquisition service, priced as a fixed annual fee or a small percentage of the incentive.
- Regulatory risk: if the 2027 tariff deadline is not extended, new CER formation stops at end-2027, while operating existing CERs continues for 20 years. Back-office operations are therefore the more durable niche.

### Gaps
- No verified CER management price lists (Regalgrid, Sorgenia B2B, ènostra).
- No 2026 GSE regional counts for Lombardia and Piemonte.
- No confirmation on gse.it of the PNRR works and operation deadlines.

## 3. Solar + storage + EV charging in condominiums (IT condominio, CH PPE/STWE)

### Takeaway
Two live, deadline-bound incentive windows exist.
- Italy: MIMIT "bonus colonnine domestiche" pays 80% of costs, up to €1,500 per person or €8,000 for condominium common parts, for works between 26 Jun and 31 Dec 2026. Applications run on Invitalia from 22 Sep 2026 to 31 Jan 2027, first-come first-served, with €15 m for 2026.
- Ticino: the cantonal decree (RL 741.280, 8 Mar 2023) pays CHF 500 per charger, from CHF 1,200 per station with dynamic load management (aimed at condominium garages), and CHF 4,000 for bidirectional. Budget CHF 7.5 m.

Small budgets and first-come rules favour a fast "condominium EV-charging project manager / application filer". Note this is adjacent to the excluded heating-incentive paperwork idea.

### Cited Findings
- Italy, MIMIT bonus colonnine 2026:
  - 80% of costs, max €1,500 (individuals) or €8,000 (condominium common parts);
  - works carried out from 26 Jun to 31 Dec 2026;
  - Invitalia portal open 22 Sep 2026 12:00 to 31 Jan 2027 12:00, processed in chronological order;
  - €68 m to 2030, of which €15 m for 2026; businesses and VAT holders excluded;
  - eligible costs include design, electrical and building works, testing and a new POD connection;
  - it is a refund, not a tax deduction; the 50%/36% renovation deduction may apply separately.
  — [idealista, 17 Sep 2026](https://www.idealista.it/news/finanza/fisco/2026/09/17/453035-bonus-colonnine-elettriche-2026-al-via-quando-e-come-fare-domanda); [alVolante](https://www.alvolante.it/news/bonus-colonnine-2026-al-via-incentivi-cittadini-e-condomini-419469); [CAF Informa](https://cafinforma.it/bonus-colonnine-domestiche-2026/); [Tuttotech, 23 Sep 2026](https://www.tuttotech.net/news/2026/09/23/bonus-colonnine-2026-privati-condomini.html)
- Ticino, cantonal decree on EVs and charging infrastructure (RL 741.280, 8 Mar 2023):
  - CHF 500 per unidirectional station; CHF 4,000 per bidirectional station;
  - dynamic load management "from CHF 1,200 per installed station", described as mainly for condominium garages and company car parks;
  - for load management, the application must be filed before works;
  - standard applications must be filed within 30 days of installation;
  - charging-system credit CHF 7.5 m; CHF 2.08 m was used in 2023 for 1,765 transitional requests, leaving CHF 5.42 m;
  - administered by SPAAS via ti.ch/incentivi; an extension was expected in early 2026 (final details unconfirmed).
  — [TicinoEnergia, 30 Sep 2025](https://www.ticinoenergia.ch/it/blog/mobilita-sostenibile-come-il-ticino-sostiene-la-transizione-elettrica.html); [RSI](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Veicoli-elettrici-il-sostegno-va-alle-ricariche--1802302.html)

### Inferences
- In a PPE or condominium, the hard part is not hardware. It is assembly approval, load-management design, splitting costs between owners, and per-user metering and billing. The same skills apply to RCP/vRCP billing (section 1), so EV charging + PV + RCP billing could be packaged as one "condominium energy manager" offer.
- Italy's €15 m for 2026 is roughly 1,900 condominium grants at the €8,000 maximum (arithmetic inference). It will likely run out fast, which favours speed of filing.

### Gaps
- No data on Ticino EV-charger demand in PPE, on installer shortages in Ticino or Lombardy, or on the status of the 2026 Ticino extension.
- No Swiss federal "right to charge" rule was verified. The Italian condominium rule (art. 1122-bis c.c.) was not fetched.

## 4. Climate adaptation: extreme events, insurance claims and protection

### Takeaway
Losses are large and recurring:
- Swiss insurers estimated CHF 160–200 m of insured losses from the June–July 2024 storms in Valais and Ticino, comparable to the Ticino hail of 25 Aug 2023;
- total Vallemaggia damage and reconstruction is about CHF 100 m;
- Mesolcina repairs exceed CHF 74 m;
- 2024 weather damage in Switzerland was about CHF 905 m (WSL);
- Italy had record insured weather losses of over €6 bn in 2023 (€5.5 bn atmospheric events), with Lombardia first for damaging events (62).

The most concrete regulatory demand driver is Italy's mandatory natural-catastrophe insurance for businesses (L. 213/2023). It has applied to micro and small firms since 1 Jan 2026, and to hospitality, food service and fishing since 31 Mar 2026. This creates demand for risk assessment, prevention measures that lower premiums, and claims assistance.

### Cited Findings
- Swiss Insurance Association (ASA/SVV) early estimate: CHF 160–200 m insured losses for mid-June to early-July 2024 storms, with Valais and Ticino hardest hit. It was described as comparable to the Ticino hailstorm of 25 Aug 2023. — [ASA/SVV press release](https://svv.ch/it/attualites/comunicato-stampa/maltempo-vallese-e-ticino-lasa-stima-chf-160-200-milioni-di-danni)
- Vallemaggia (June 2024): about CHF 100 m in damage and reconstruction, CHF 44.5 m borne by the Canton; the Canton prepared CHF 25 m for upper Vallemaggia. Mesolcina repairs are over CHF 74 m (versus 38 m initially expected). WSL put 2024 Swiss weather damage at about CHF 905 m, about 85% from June events. — [laRegione](https://www.laregione.ch/cantone/locarnese/1870645/danni-inondazioni-detritiche-colate-vallemaggia); [tio.ch](https://www.tio.ch/ticino/attualita/1840263/alta-vallemaggia-pronti-25-milioni-di-franchi-dal-cantone); [Ticinonews, two years on](https://www.ticinonews.ch/ticino/alluvione-in-vallemaggia-due-anni-di-interventi-e-ricostruzione-437329); [RSI two years on](https://www.rsi.ch/info/ticino-grigioni-e-insubria/Vallemaggia-due-anni-dopo-tra-ferite-aperte-e-rimarginate--3855749.html)
- Italy, ANIA: record insured losses of over €6 bn in 2023, €5.5 bn from extreme weather and about €0.8 bn from the Emilia-Romagna and Tuscany floods. — [QuiFinanza](https://quifinanza.it/attualita/danni-maltempo-2023-ania/831504/); [Renewable Matter](https://www.renewablematter.eu/danni-catastrofali-italia-2023-record-6-miliardi-euro-quelli-assicurati)
- Lombardia, July 2023 hail: hail claims frequency rose by 30% in July; average car body repair cost €5,000–6,000; CNA Carrozzieri reported more than 30% more repair jobs in affected areas. — [SaronnoNews](https://www.saronnonews.it/2023/08/03/dopo-le-grandinate-a-luglio-30-di-interventi-riparatori-in-carrozzeria/?output=pdf); [Il Giorno](https://www.ilgiorno.it/cronaca/grandina-auto-carrozzieri-seq4a3r6)
- Legambiente/Unipol CittàClima 2023: Lombardia ranked first with 62 damaging weather and hydrological events. 24 July 2023 was the worst hail day, with a storm forming over Lombardia. — [Legambiente CittaClima 2023](https://www.legambiente.it/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Bilancio-CittaClima-2023.pdf); [Focus](https://www.focus.it/ambiente/natura/2023-anno-nero-grandine-italia-europa)
- Italy mandatory cat-nat business insurance (L. 213/2023, Art. 1 c.101 ff.):
  - large firms: 31 Mar 2025;
  - medium firms: 1 Oct 2025;
  - micro and small firms: 31 Dec 2025, i.e. from 1 Jan 2026;
  - Milleproroghe moved hospitality, food service and fishing/aquaculture to 31 Mar 2026;
  - agriculture is under AGRICAT; IVASS sanctions breaches of insurers' obligation to offer cover.
  — [Artser](https://www.artser.it/approfondimenti/proroga-obbligo-polizze-catastrofali-le-nuove-scadenze-per-le-imprese.html); [Eutekne](https://www.eutekne.info/Sezioni/Art_1080771_rinvio_in_extremis_dell_obbligo_di_polizza_catastrofale_per_alcuni.aspx); [Finera guide 2026](https://finera.it/articoli/news/polizza-catastrofale-guida-definitiva-imprese/); [Brokersicuro](https://www.brokersicuro.it/guida-catastrofale.html)

### Inferences
- In Switzerland, building insurance against natural hazards is broadly covered: Ticino has no cantonal monopoly insurer, so private insurers cover it. Claims handling is done by insurers and loss adjusters, so a solo "claims assistant" faces a narrow market. More promising: prevention and adaptation services after events, such as hail-resistant materials (HW class), flood barriers, property hazard checks against cantonal hazard maps, and PV hail-damage inspection (drone and thermography). These are inferences; no demand data was found.
- In Italy, the mandatory policy for about 4+ million micro and small firms (firm count is general knowledge, not sourced here) creates a recurring need for:
  - a simple "risk dossier" per site (flood, hail and landslide exposure, prevention measures) for comparing quotes and negotiating premiums;
  - post-event claims documentation.
  Insurance brokers are natural partners or channels. Selling the policy itself requires RUI registration (inference).

### Gaps

## 5. Energy audits and certification: CECE (CH) and APE (IT)

### Takeaway
In Ticino the CECE is required for cantonal energy-renovation subsidies from CHF 10,000. It costs about CHF 300–600 (CECE) or CHF 600–1,200 (CECE Plus with advisory report), is valid 10 years, and cantons often subsidise about half. No official evidence was found of a Ticino CECE obligation at sale. In Italy the APE is mandatory for sales and rentals and typically costs €130–300, a commoditised, crowded market.

### Cited Findings
- CECE is mandatory in Ticino to apply for cantonal renovation subsidies from CHF 10,000. Costs: CECE about CHF 300–600, CECE Plus about CHF 600–1,200, both valid 10 years. Some cantons and communes subsidise CECE/CECE Plus. Source is a private provider. — [ADT energy contractor](https://adtenergycontractor.ch/cose-il-cece/)
- UBS: the CECE is required in some cantons for property sales; Ticino is not named. — [UBS GEAK explained](https://www.ubs.com/ch/it/services/guide/mortgages-and-financing/articles/geak-and-minergie-explained.html)
- Italy APE: mandatory for sales and rentals; typical price range €130–300 per search results; widely offered online. — [Infobuild Energia APE 2026](https://www.infobuildenergia.it/approfondimenti/attestato-di-prestazione-energetica-definizione-normativa-e-costi/); [APEfacile](https://www.apefacile.it/news/infoape/certificato-ape-per-compravendita-quanto-costa-e-chi-lo-paga/); [BibLus](https://biblus.acca.it/ape-obbligatorio/)

### Inferences
- Issuing a CECE requires accreditation as a CECE expert. The CECE overlaps with the already-recommended heating-incentive paperwork service: it is a prerequisite step. So it is better treated as an add-on or partnership (refer to accredited experts) than as a new standalone niche.
- APE in Italy is commoditised and requires a qualified technician, so it is a weak opening for a non-technician founder.

### Gaps
- No official ti.ch confirmation of CECE requirements or of the number of CECE issued in Ticino.
- No count of certified CECE experts in Ticino.
- The effect of the EU EPBD "case green" transposition on APE demand was not researched.
