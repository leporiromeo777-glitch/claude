More than a ride — a chauffeur who works around you

A private driver is not a taxi. Your personal chauffeur in Zurich plans the route in advance, waits while you dine or shop, adapts when your plans change and treats every detail — doors, luggage, timing — as part of the service.

Many of our clients book a professional chauffeur regularly: for the school run, for weekly commutes between Zurich and Geneva, or as a 24 hour chauffeur during their stay in Switzerland. Tell us what your days look like, and we build the service around them.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a private driver and a taxi?
A private driver is booked in advance, waits for you at every stop, adapts to your schedule and drives a premium vehicle. You get one professional chauffeur dedicated to you — not the next car in a queue.
Can I have the same personal driver every time?
Yes. On request we assign you a regular chauffeur who knows your preferences and routes — many clients travel with the same driver for years.
Do you drive outside Zurich?
Of course. Your private driver takes you anywhere in Switzerland — Geneva, Lucerne, St. Moritz, Basel — and to neighbouring countries on request.
How far in advance should I book a private driver?
A few hours are usually enough, and we are available 24/7 for last-minute requests. For full days or busy weekends we recommend booking one or two days ahead.
